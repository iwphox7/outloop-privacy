# Outloop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Outloop ("the app") is a puzzle game published by iwphox. This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Information we collect

We do not run any server for the app and we do not ask you to create an account. Your game progress, stars, streaks and settings are stored only on your device.

## Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and approximate location to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. This data is handled by Google under its own policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

## Sharing

If you tap Share after a level, the app opens your device's share sheet with a short text message. You choose where it goes. We do not receive it.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
